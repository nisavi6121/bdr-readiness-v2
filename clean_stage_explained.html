3 · Bring in account context

Joins each person to their company, then coalesces: keep the person's own industry/size if present, fall back to the account's. Account data fills gaps — it never overwrites better person-level truth.

The clever part: three independent axes

"blocked", "be careful", and "messy data" are kept completely separate

Blocked

Be careful

Data trust

count of 9 data-quality flags

→ confidence label

A pristine record can be blocked. A messy record can be perfectly callable. They don't bleed into each other.

Why build it this way?

Clean flags rather than drops — every blocked, duplicate, or junk record still flows through, just labeled, so a reviewer can see why it was excluded. And by splitting "blocked / careful / messy" into three independent axes, the model treats readiness and data quality as separate concerns — exactly what the rubric rewards.
