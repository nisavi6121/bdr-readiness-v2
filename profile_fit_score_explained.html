2 Missing values → within-type median

This is the one component that explicitly imputes. Missing level/persona is filled with the median of that person's own entity type (leads and contacts separately), computed on the derived score, with a 0.30 floor.

job_level missingpersona missing
Leads (600)75 (~12%)248 (~41%)
Contacts (400)66 (~17%)152 (~38%)

~40% of persona is blank — a global mean would unfairly judge sparse leads against richer contacts. Per-type median keeps it fair.

Sentinel, not missing: Non-Prospects map to a real 0.0 (not NaN), so the median never lifts them. "I know they're a competitor" ≠ "I don't know who they are."
3 Non-prospect override (at scoring)

For any Non-Prospect, both account fit and profile fit are forced to 0 at score time — internal consistency, since they're hard-blocked anyway (304 records).
