When a suppressed lead outscored its contact, the contact inherits the higher tier — signal isn't lost.

3 Tier ≠ Action — two different questions
tier
the score bucket — how ready, by the numbers
action_tag
what to actually do — accounts for suppression & channel
A suppressed lead can be Call Now tier  but  Converted action → "work the promoted contact."

That's why Flagged (369) ≠ Blocked tag (309): some flagged records are also converted, so they're tagged "Converted." Score and action are separate.
